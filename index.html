Yuqiao Tan

谭宇乔 · Master’s Student

I'm a master’s student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Shizhu He. I received my B.E. from UESTC.

tanyuqiao2025[at]ia.ac.cn GitHub Google Scholar

My research focuses on language models and AI agents, especially how agents can improve through closed-loop learning and become better collaborators for real users. My interests include:

  • User collaborator agents from real user interaction
  • Long-horizon tasks, model capability improvement, and training stability
  • Benchmark design and execution-based assessment
  • Data synthesis, environment generation, and post-training

I have worked on large-scale optimization of foundation models to improve how they interact and collaborate with users.
